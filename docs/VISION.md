# Vision

**Own the models you use: find open-weight models, run them on your own hardware, understand them, and know what every answer costs.**

## Sovereign

The weights are files on your disk. Inference runs on your CPU and GPU. No prompt, completion, model file, or usage record leaves the machine. The network exists for two things, the catalogue and downloads, and the app shows every byte it fetched and where it put it. This is the opposite of a cloud AI client: nothing here depends on a key, a subscription, or a company staying in business.

## Three stages

- **Worm (now).** One machine, four capabilities: a hardware report that says what fits; search and download from Hugging Face; run and chat with a downloaded model, with every parameter explained and presets you can keep; benchmarks per category with energy per answer. A dependable tool for one person who wants to learn AI engineering by touching the knobs.
- **Cocoon (undefined).** The owner's other machines join over Tailscale: send a model or a benchmark job to another device, keep one catalogue across them, compare the same model on different hardware. Nothing in the cocoon is scheduled; it is named so the worm does not grow sideways into it.
- **Butterfly (vision).** For anyone who wants to own their models. Vision and audio models through the same engine; more sources than Hugging Face; benchmark categories for code, images, audio and science; comparisons you can publish because every number carries its provenance.

## Who it is for

First the owner. Then anyone who wants to own their models rather than rent them, and who wants to understand what a temperature, a context window, a quantisation level or a joule actually means by measuring it on their own machine.

## What it is not

- **Not a cloud AI client.** No API keys for hosted models, ever.
- **Not a model host.** It serves no one else, has no accounts, and never redistributes weights.
- **Not a chat product.** Chat is the workbench where parameters are tried and presets are kept. The product is choosing and measuring.
